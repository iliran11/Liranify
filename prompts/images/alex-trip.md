# Alex's Ecuador Journey - Visual Infographic Prompt

## 🎯 Goal

Describe Alex's trip as a visual "journey" infographic.

Create a detailed and accurate visual representation of the route and central events.

## 👤 Role

- Visual artist
- Professional infographic designer
- Expert in visualizing travel data

## 🖥️ Image Format

**The Layered Timeline:** Format the image as a horizontal timeline with the travel dates running across the bottom axis.

The locations (Waypoints 1-6) should be stacked vertically above the timeline, with distinct visual icons or small illustrations representing the key events and activities corresponding to each time block.

### Style Requirements

- Primary output must be a single, detailed infographic image
- Use a hand-drawn, cheerful, and slightly whimsical illustration style (similar to travel posters or illustrated maps)
- No header or title text should appear in the infographic

### Language

- **Hebrew** - Generate the infographic in Hebrew language. All text, labels, and captions should be in Hebrew.

### Special Image Instructions

- Give disproportionately more space for Montañita in the timeline
- Absolutely highlight the surfing vibes in Montañita sections

## 👥 Audience

- Anyone interested in Alex's story
- Viewers seeking a visual illustration of travel

## 🗺️ Journey Data (Waypoints)

### Waypoint 1: Tel Aviv, Israel → Guayaquil, Ecuador

- **Date:** January 8
- **Journey Details:** International flight
- **Accommodation:** One night at a luxury hotel - Holiday Inn

### Waypoint 2: Guayaquil → Montañita, Ecuador

- **Dates:** January 9 - January 15 (7 nights)
- **Journey Details:** Land travel (bus/taxi) along the coast
- **Accommodation:** 7 nights in Montañita

### Waypoint 3: Montañita → Galapagos Islands, Ecuador

- **Dates:** January 16 - January 22 (7 nights)
- **Journey Details:** Flight
- **Accommodation:** 7 nights in the islands

### Waypoint 4: Galapagos Islands → Baños de Agua Santa, Ecuador

- **Dates:** January 23 - January 25 (3 nights)
- **Journey Details:** Flight back to the mainland and land travel to the mountain region
- **Accommodation:** 3 nights in Baños

### Waypoint 5: Baños → Montañita, Ecuador (Return)

- **Dates:** January 26 - January 30 (5 nights)
- **Journey Details:** Long land travel back to the coast
- **Accommodation:** 5 additional nights in Montañita

### Waypoint 6: Montañita, Ecuador → Tel Aviv, Israel (Conclusion)

- **Date:** January 31
- **Journey Details:** Travel to airport + international return flight
